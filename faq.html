General Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Splitzon.

What is Splitzon?▾

Splitzon is a simple and smart app to share expenses with friends, roommates, family, and travel groups. You can create groups, add expenses, and let Splitzon handle the math — so you can focus on the people, not the payments.

Is Splitzon free to use?▾

Yes, Splitzon is free to use for all core features like creating groups, adding expenses, and settling balances. A Premium plan is available for advanced features.

Do I need to create an account?▾

Yes, a free account is required so your groups and expenses are saved and synced across devices.

Which platforms is Splitzon available on?▾

Splitzon is available on iOS, Android, and the Web, with your data staying in sync across all of them.

Is my data safe with Splitzon?▾

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to keep your personal and financial data secure at all times.

Can I use Splitzon internationally?▾

Yes, Splitzon supports multiple currencies so you can use it while traveling or splitting expenses abroad.

How do I get support if I face an issue?▾

You can reach our support team anytime through the Contact Support button below or by emailing support@splitzon.com.

Groups & Members

Get answers about creating groups, managing members, invitations, permissions and more.

How do I create a group in Splitzon?▾

To create a group, tap the "+" button on the home screen and select "Create Group". Give your group a name, add a description (optional), and invite members. You can start adding expenses right away!

How do I invite members to my group?▾

Open the group, tap "Invite Members", and share the invite link via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.

Is there a limit on the number of members?▾

Free groups support up to 10 members. Premium removes this limit entirely.

Can I remove a member from a group?▾

Yes, group admins can remove a member from the group settings page at any time.

Can I transfer group ownership?▾

Yes, go to group settings, select a member, and choose "Make Admin" to transfer ownership.

What permissions do group members have?▾

Members can add expenses, view balances, and settle payments. Only admins can remove members or delete the group.

Can I leave a group?▾

Yes, you can leave a group anytime from group settings, as long as your balance is settled.

What happens to expenses if a member leaves?▾

Their past expenses remain in the group history, but they won't be included in future splits.

Adding & Splitting Expenses

Get answers about adding expenses, splitting bills, using different split methods, and managing expense details.

How do I add an expense in Splitzon?▾
  1. Tap the "+" button on the home screen.
  2. Enter the amount, a title (e.g., Dinner, Rent, Trip), and select a group.
  3. Choose a category (e.g., Food, Travel, Shopping).
  4. Select how you want to split (equally, by amount, or by percentage).
  5. Add members and tap "Save".

That's it! The expense will be added and shared with group members.

What are the different split methods available?▾

You can split equally, by exact amount, by percentage, or by shares.

Can I split an expense unequally?▾

Yes, choose "By Amount" or "By Percentage" when adding the expense to split it unequally.

How do I edit or delete an expense?▾

Open the expense from the group, tap the three-dot menu, and choose Edit or Delete.

Can I add a receipt or image to an expense?▾

Yes, you can attach a photo of the receipt while adding or editing an expense.

What categories can I use?▾

Splitzon offers categories like Food, Travel, Rent, Shopping, Entertainment, and more.

Will members get notified when I add an expense?▾

Yes, all group members receive an instant notification when a new expense is added.

Can I add expenses in different currencies?▾

Yes, Splitzon supports multiple currencies for international groups and trips.

What happens if I added the wrong amount?▾

You can edit the expense anytime to correct the amount — balances update automatically.

Can I mark an expense as paid?▾

Yes, you can mark any expense or your share as paid manually from the expense details screen.

Payments & Settlements

Get answers about settling balances, making payments, tracking who owes what, and using different payment methods in Splitzon.

How do I settle my balance in Splitzon?▾
  1. Open the group and go to the Settlements tab.
  2. Tap "Settle Now" next to the amount you owe.
  3. Choose a payment method (e.g., UPI, card, or mark as paid offline).
  4. Confirm the payment and you're done!

Once the payment is completed, the balance will be updated automatically for all members in the group.

What payment methods are supported?▾

Splitzon supports UPI, debit/credit cards, and marking payments as done offline.

Is it safe to make payments through Splitzon?▾

Yes, all payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways.

Can I mark a payment as done manually?▾

Yes, if you paid someone outside the app (e.g., cash), you can mark it as settled manually.

How long does it take for a payment to reflect?▾

Online payments reflect instantly. Manual settlements reflect as soon as you mark them.

What happens after I make a payment?▾

The group balance updates automatically and all members are notified.

Can I view my payment history?▾

Yes, tap on any group and go to the Settlements tab to view full payment history.

Can I remind someone to settle their balance?▾

Yes, tap "Remind" next to their name to send a gentle payment reminder.

Does Splitzon charge any fees for payments?▾

Splitzon does not charge extra fees; standard payment gateway charges may apply.

Account & Security

Get answers about your account, login, security settings, data privacy, and keeping your information safe on Splitzon.

How do I create an account on Splitzon?▾
  1. Download the Splitzon app (iOS or Android) or visit our website.
  2. Enter your mobile number and verify it with the OTP.
  3. Add your name and profile details.
  4. You're all set! Start creating groups and sharing expenses.
How do I log in to my account?▾

Enter your registered mobile number and verify with the OTP sent to your phone.

Can I change my registered mobile number?▾

Yes, go to Profile → Security → Change Mobile Number and verify the new number with OTP.

How can I reset my password?▾

Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen and follow the OTP verification steps.

Is my data safe with Splitzon?▾

Yes, we use industry-leading encryption to protect all your personal and financial data.

Can I delete my account?▾

Yes, go to Profile → Security → Delete Account. This action is permanent.

What data does Splitzon collect?▾

We collect only what's needed — your name, mobile number, and expense data you enter.

Can I control who sees my information?▾

Yes, use the Privacy settings to control what group members can see about you.

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?▾

Go to Profile → Security → Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup steps.

What should I do if I suspect suspicious activity?▾

Immediately change your password and contact support@splitzon.com.
